The product

Less chasing records. More clarity across the fleet.

FlightOps is being developed to bring together the information drone teams need to plan and review fleet operations. Contact Avistech to discuss the product direction.

01 / FLIGHT LOGS

Keep each flight in context

Record aircraft, date, duration, pilot, and notes in one proposed digital logbook workflow.

02 / SERVICE TRACKING

See maintenance coming

Plan service intervals and keep maintenance notes associated with the aircraft they belong to.

03 / ASSISTED INSIGHTS

Explore AI-assisted summaries

Get a clearer view of fleet records with assisted summaries. Operational decisions remain with qualified people.

FlightOps is in development. It does not replace manufacturer instructions, required records, inspections, or applicable aviation rules.
