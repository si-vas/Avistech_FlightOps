Website information

Privacy notice

Last reviewed: October 19, 2026

This notice describes the current early-access website. Update it if the website, signup flow, or product data handling changes. Review it for the laws that apply to your business before collecting visitor information.

Early-access signup

If you submit the signup form and consent to updates, Avistech sends your email address to Resend to deliver a confirmation. A copy of the message is sent to contactus@avistech.space so Avistech can receive and manage the request. The signup form does not currently write to an application database. The request and address may remain in the recipient mailboxes and email provider systems.

Hosting and technical information

The website is intended to be hosted on Vercel. Vercel may process technical request information, including IP address and browser details, under its own terms. The signup endpoint applies a basic in-memory request limit; it does not create a persistent visitor profile.

Use and removal requests

The email address is used to confirm the request and provide FlightOps launch updates. To stop future updates or ask us to remove your request from our email records, write to contactus@avistech.space. Copies already delivered may remain in email provider backups or logs according to their retention practices.

Workspace preview

The workspace page contains fictional sample information. It does not accept, save, or transmit actual flight, aircraft, pilot, or maintenance records.

Questions

For privacy questions, email contactus@avistech.space.
