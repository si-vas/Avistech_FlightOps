Website information

Privacy notice

Last reviewed: October 19, 2026

This notice describes the current contact form and website. Update it if the website or product data handling changes. Review it for the laws that apply to your business before collecting visitor information.

Contact enquiries

If you submit the contact form, Avistech receives your first and last name, email address, company, selected country calling code, phone number, enquiry topic, and message. The information is sent through Resend to contactus@avistech.space so Avistech can review and answer your enquiry. The service attempts to send a confirmation to the email address you provide. Contact submissions are not currently written to an application database; copies may remain in mailboxes and email-provider systems.

Hosting and technical information

The website is intended to be hosted on Vercel. Vercel may process technical request information, including IP address and browser details, under its own terms. The contact endpoint applies a basic in-memory request limit; it does not create a persistent visitor profile.

Use and removal requests

Your contact details are used to respond to your enquiry. To ask us to remove your request from our email records, write to contactus@avistech.space. Copies already delivered may remain in email provider backups or logs according to their retention practices.

Workspace preview

The workspace page contains fictional sample information. It does not accept, save, or transmit actual flight, aircraft, pilot, or maintenance records.

Questions

For privacy questions, email contactus@avistech.space.
