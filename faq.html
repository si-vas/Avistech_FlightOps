Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about FlightOps.

Product

What is FlightOps?

FlightOps is Avistech’s product in development: a planned cloud-based flight log and maintenance tracking workspace for commercial drone operators and flight schools.

Can I create an account or record real flights now?

No. The service is not currently open for accounts or operational flight records. The workspace visuals on this site are illustrative only.

Does FlightOps currently use AI?

No AI service is connected to this website. AI-assisted summaries are a planned capability, subject to product design and human review.

Can FlightOps replace required aviation records or inspections?

No. The product is not available for operational use and is not a substitute for applicable rules, required records, manufacturer instructions, qualified maintenance, or inspections.

Plans and data

Are the listed prices available to purchase?

Not yet. The $29, $79, and $249 monthly amounts are planned pricing and may change before launch. No subscription checkout is currently available.

What is included in the Enterprise storage plan?

Dedicated cloud storage is a planned capability only. Architecture, access controls, retention, backups, and availability are not finalized.

What information does the contact form collect?

The form requests your name, email, company, country calling code, phone number, enquiry topic, and message. The details are sent to Avistech by email through our configured email provider. See the privacy notice for details.

How do I request deletion or stop email?

Contact contactus@avistech.space and ask us to remove your enquiry or stop further contact.
